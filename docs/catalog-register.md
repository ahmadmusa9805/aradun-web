## Frozen business behavior

English/USD initially; Get a Quote primary, Contact visible, consultation distinct. Quote steps: Service -> Project -> Contact -> Review; editable canonical selections and custom/unsure path. Server resolves offers and preserves original inquiry snapshots; no binding automatic total or P1 checkout. Manual consultation request is not an appointment.

Sales stages: New -> Qualified -> Discovery -> Proposal Sent -> Negotiation -> Won / Lost. Spam/duplicate/no-response are inquiry classifications; Won is not Paid. Contact holds the customer profile; customer conversion is explicit and history-preserving. Manual follow-up tasks remain P1; external campaigns and configurable pipelines remain future.

The ten promoted P1 capabilities are testimonials, articles/resource center, FAQ/help, article management, SEO metadata editing, inquiry source tracking, website search, manual follow-up tasks, opportunity pipeline and lead-to-customer conversion. Article publishing does not authorize broad CMS. Catalog/core/portfolio remain source-managed; Article/Testimonial/SeoOverride are the limited database-managed CMS baseline; FAQ editor is conditional.

P1 excludes client portal, gateway/checkout, online signing, full project suite, full CMS/media library, campaign engine, AMS, advanced AI and tenancy. Industries/Products retain P2 baseline; Product Terms follows its approved P2/P3 product release. Basic security and reliability accompany any first feature.
