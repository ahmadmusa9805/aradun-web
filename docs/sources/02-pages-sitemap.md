### 1.3 Inventory conventions

- Each inventory record has a unique page ID, name, URL or route pattern, phase, access, status, indexing treatment, purpose, target user, placement, parent/children, action and requirement references.
- A route pattern such as `/insights/[slug]` serves many published records. Brackets identify parameters; they are not literal public URL text.
- Parent/child relationships are information hierarchy. They need not mirror every path segment. Related content links are not child pages.
- `ROOT`, `STA-ROOT`, `CLI-ROOT` and `AMS-ROOT` are grouping nodes, not pages.
- Page IDs `PUB-S###` map to `SVC-###`. Page IDs `SOL-01`–`SOL-05` map to master offer IDs `SOL-001`–`SOL-005`; these are different namespaces.
- “Index” means eligible once approved, substantive and published, never a guarantee of search inclusion. Noindex is not an access-control mechanism.
- All private records require server-side role and record authorization. Listed roles are proposed responsibility scopes; final D-013 permissions remain unresolved.

### 4.1 Private module boundaries and shared views

- Leads are inquiries, contacts are shared people identities, opportunities are commercial records, and customers are staff-confirmed customer records/classifications. Separate views do not authorize four duplicated identity stores. D-011 governs linking, reviewed merges and conversion corrections.
- The P1 sales stages are fixed: New → Qualified → Discovery → Proposal Sent → Negotiation → Won / Lost. Spam, duplicate and no-response remain secondary classifications. Won does not mean paid. Drag-and-drop and multiple configurable pipelines are not launch requirements.
- P1 follow-up tasks are manual with due/overdue dashboard reminders. Automated external campaigns, sequences and scheduled reminders remain later.
- P1 article drafting/publishing/unpublishing is confirmed. General service/page/solution editing, media library and broader publishing are P2 proposals. The SEO editor can edit metadata without granting general page editing.
- FAQ/help content must be maintained, but a P1 dashboard authoring interface remains D-012. If developer-maintained content is chosen, ADM-021/022 are not launched; public FAQ/search requirements remain intact.
- Minimal P1 staff provisioning/revocation is required. ADM-023/024 are conditional UI allocations; a selected provider's protected administration can satisfy the capability without a new P1 team suite. Broader team/department management remains P2.
- Audit recording is required from launch. ADM-025 is an optional launch viewer, not permission to defer logging. Likewise basic monitoring, retry visibility and incident handling do not wait for P3 consoles.
- ADM-027 is specifically unresolved under D-007. An approved external ledger may replace the dashboard tracker; there is no public P1 payment route. P1 proposal/invoice templates and manual commercial operations do not require P2 document software.
- EXP-004 groups tasks/subtasks, team assignment, milestones, task board, files/documentation, deadlines, deliverable review, client-submitted requirements/changes/messages and time tracking into authorized tabs. P3 adds profitability/Gantt/AI support only when approved. CLI-003 exposes only client-permitted views of the same engagement.
- Proposals, contracts, invoice records and project approvals are shared business capabilities with separate authorized staff/client presentations. Signing and payment can use approved providers; no arbitrary anonymous document access is introduced.
- The client portal supports own-project progress, intake, files, version-specific acceptance, change requests, messages, billing and support as mapped. A client cannot see internal notes, other clients or unauthorized finance records.
- `/ams` and descendants are reserved information-architecture candidates for a separately approved product/module. Final hostname, application boundary and agency identity model are unresolved. They do not imply that a commercial AMS currently exists or that ARADUN's marketing depends on it. Single-agency scope is possible; TENANT is required only if multi-organization scope is selected.

## 8. URL conventions, route resolution and authentication boundaries

### 8.1 URL naming

- Use lowercase, descriptive, hyphen-separated public slugs. Display names retain their official spelling even when URLs use simplified words, such as `ecommerce` or `ui-ux`.
- Use `https://aradun.com` as the proposed canonical origin matching the master domain. Canonical www/non-www redirect configuration is a launch verification item. Choose one trailing-slash policy; recommended routes here use no trailing slash except `/`.
- Preserve the existing flat service-route constraint: both category and service details use `/services/[slug]`. One catalog resolver distinguishes category and service entities. Do not introduce competing `/services/[categorySlug]` and `/services/[serviceSlug]` definitions at the same path level, or silently migrate to `/services/category/service`.
- Keep category slugs and service slugs globally disjoint within `/services/`. Validate against every active, reserved and retired redirect slug before publishing. The eight category slugs and all 57 proposed service slugs in this file are distinct.
- `/solutions/[slug]`, `/our-work/[slug]`, `/insights/[slug]` and `/faq/[slug]` use controlled published record slugs. Conditional `/industries/[slug]` and `/products/[slug]` follow the same publication principle.
- Reserve static path segments such as `new`, `preview`, `advanced`, `custom`, `executions`, `api-access` and `marketplace` wherever the inventory uses them. Dynamic private identifiers must not collide with a static segment. Provider integration keys come from an approved bounded registry.
- Use stable internal record identifiers for private detail routes. An identifier or human-readable reference is never sufficient authorization. Avoid emails, names, phone numbers, inquiry text and secrets in URLs.
- `?q=` is a search query, not a separate route for each search. Query parameters for selected offers, filters and tracking do not create new page IDs. Preserve only validated context; never trust browser-submitted price amounts. Quote steps are form state, not four independently indexable pages.
- A page's stable ID and an offer's stable ID survive approved slug changes. Keep a redirect register when changing a published public URL; do not create redirect chains or redirect unrelated removed content indiscriminately to Home.
- Names of specific articles, cases, help articles and products are assigned only from approved content. There is no fixed finite total of dynamic pages.

### 8.2 App Router guidance without implementation

Public, staff, client and conditional agency areas may be organized by App Router route groups/layout boundaries later. Route-group names do not add URL segments. Do not create source files, Next.js folders, schemas or code from this document alone. A layout boundary is useful for navigation but does not replace authorization at every data operation. Express APIs, callbacks, downloads and webhook endpoints are technical interfaces, not user-facing page inventory entries.

### 8.3 Access boundaries

| Surface | Access and behavior |
|---|---|
| Public marketing and inquiry pages | Anonymous browsing/submission allowed; server validation, spam controls and privacy requirements apply |
| Staff auth gateways | Publicly reachable authentication shell; no private data; no public staff signup; provider-supported recovery/MFA |
| `/dashboard` and descendants | Valid staff session plus approved role/record permission; deny direct unauthorized requests and sensitive cached responses |
| Future client auth gateways | Identity flow only; invitation/signup decision unresolved; no business record exposure |
| `/portal` and descendants | Valid client identity plus own permitted account/project/document access |
| Conditional `/ams` | Separate agency authorization; tenant boundaries only if multi-organization scope is approved |
| Draft/preview content | Authorized editor-only delivery; no public search, sitemap or shared public cache exposure |
| Private files/documents | Access-controlled delivery; public asset URLs cannot bypass document permissions |

Unauthenticated users may be redirected to the correct sign-in flow with a validated local return destination; authenticated but unauthorized users receive a safe denial without revealing record data. Password reset/invitation tokens must follow the chosen provider's secure handling and must not enter analytics or logs. Phase-disabled routes remain unavailable and unlinked, rather than showing a fabricated working module.

## 9. SEO, canonicals and publication visibility

- Public indexable content uses a consistent self-referencing canonical, accurate metadata and internal links to the preferred URL. Include only approved, published, canonical, indexable public destinations in the XML sitemap.
- A service's category page and substantive service detail have different purposes and each may be self-canonical. Do not canonicalize every service to its category. Cross-listed SVC-009 has only one URL.
- Search results, quote utility variants, confirmation pages, authentication, staff/client/agency pages and private previews are excluded from XML sitemaps. Public noindex pages must expose their noindex instruction to crawlers; robots blocking alone is not removal or access control. Protected content remains protected even if crawlers cannot reach its noindex metadata.
- Internal search results are useful to users but not a new indexable landing-page family. Avoid generating unlimited indexable combinations of tags, filters, search terms, pricing selections or tracking parameters.
- Tracking-only copies of an otherwise identical public page point to the clean canonical. Genuinely distinct pagination must not all be falsely canonicalized to the first page; choose the final pagination behavior during architecture/content implementation.
- Short FAQ anchors are not independent canonical pages. A substantial help detail page contains distinct content, not a copy of an identical article published under Insights. Choose one primary home for each content item and link to it from other hubs.
- Publish/unpublish must update public route visibility, public search, navigation references, sitemap membership and caches consistently. Drafts are never publicly exposed just because a slug is guessable.
- Missing/unpublished content returns a defined safe not-found behavior; failed rendering does not masquerade as a successful empty page. Removed published pages receive an appropriate removal/redirect decision based on actual replacement content.
- Use truthful Organization, Service, Article and BreadcrumbList structured data where appropriate to approved content. Do not claim certification, fake reviews, nonexistent offices or guaranteed rich-result eligibility.
- Future translations require approved languages, substantive translated content, language navigation and appropriate language/canonical relationships. No locale URLs are committed now. Future currency display and personalization need not create duplicate pages.
- Public cookie/privacy/terms content reflects actual entity, providers, data practices and policies. Indexability here is a discoverability recommendation, not a legal compliance finding.

Technical reference retained from the chat's verification: [Google canonical guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/consolidate-duplicate-urls) and [Google noindex guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/block-indexing). These references support the indexing rules; the master and owner decisions govern business scope.
