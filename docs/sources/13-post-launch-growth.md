### 14.2 Jurisdiction review before activation

This is an operating checklist for review, not a conclusion that a particular campaign is lawful. Rules depend on recipient type, channel, data and actual jurisdictions. Recheck authoritative guidance at activation; ICO flags parts of its guidance as under review [E5].

| Destination | Verified guidance and practical gate |
|---|---|
| US | FTC guidance applies CAN-SPAM requirements to commercial email, including accurate headers/subjects, advertising identification, a valid physical postal address and opt-out handling [E4]. | UK | ICO distinguishes corporate subscribers from sole traders/certain partnerships; personal-data use still needs the appropriate basis and transparency.
