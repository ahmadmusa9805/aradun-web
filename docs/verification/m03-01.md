## Implemented acceptance evidence

- Fixed `aradun-local` provider and immutable credential subject establish identity. Matching email under another provider never grants access; inactive/unprovisioned users are denied.
- Maintained Passport/Passport-Local Mongoose credentials and express-session are the local composition. Express is the single session authority; Passport does not persist another session.
- Source-shaped StaffUser/StaffSession models, hashed session IDs, current active/accessVersion checks, 30-minute idle and eight-hour absolute expiry, revocation and logout are tested against disposable MongoDB.
- Login/logout and safe local return work. Only the implemented `/staff/login` destination is currently allowed; arbitrary/external or future destinations fall back there.
- `/staff/login` provides accessible email/password, generic failure feedback, preserved email, cleared password, explicit password-only status and confirmed logout. Noindex metadata, three light themes and narrow/mobile views are tested.
- Cookie scope, Origin/custom-header checks, bounded strict JSON, explicit shared login limits and fail-closed database/index readiness are implemented. Store generic writes cannot create/promote sessions. Password-only sessions grant no MFA-protected business access.

The M03-01 local acceptance criteria are met. This does not claim M03-02 record authorization, M03-03 MFA/recovery, M03-04 real provisioning/lifecycle, M03-05 production/adversarial acceptance or release readiness.
